The Arena Awaits:
Where Mortals Play,
Legends Walk Off.

Forged in 2022 by the audacious minds of the STME Impulse Sports & Cultural Committee at NMIMS Hyderabad, the championship was engineered for raw collegiate dominance. We dismantled conventional campus meets to craft a blood-and-sweat colosseum of fierce inter-department and inter-collegiate rivalries.

Spanning the vast collegiate campus at Jadcherla, Hyderabad, athletes clash across our floodlit international-spec cricket grounds and professional astroturf football pitch. Here, glory is not shared—it is conquered. With precision VAR telemetry, professional commentary broadcasts, and over 4,000 screaming partisans, only champions dictate who walks off triumphant.
