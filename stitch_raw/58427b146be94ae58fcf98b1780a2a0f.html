The Arena Awaits:
Legends Walk Off

Forged in the heart of NMIMS Hyderabad by the School of Technology Management & Engineering (STME) Impulse Sports Committee, Legends Walk Off is not simply a collegiate tournament. It is a crucible where raw varsity rivalry transforms ordinary student athletes into immortal legends under blinding floodlights.

Spanning both floodlit T20-style cricket battles and aggressive full-pitch 11-a-side football clashes, the Jadcherla campus grounds host the fiercest tactical minds and stamina powerhouses across India. There are no draws. There is no retreat. Only legends walk off victorious.
